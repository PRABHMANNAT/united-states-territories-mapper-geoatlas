# Code of conduct

Keep project spaces welcoming to participants of all backgrounds. Discuss disagreements with evidence and address the work directly. Respect personal boundaries and keep private information confidential.

Harassment, targeted insults, discrimination, threats, and publishing someone’s private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove abusive material and restrict participation to protect contributors.

Use GitHub’s private reporting options for conduct incidents involving a repository or account. Provide enough context for review and avoid publishing sensitive details in a public issue.
