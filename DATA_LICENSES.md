# Dataset licensing and attribution

| Dataset | Reuse and attribution |
| --- | --- |
| U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line, cartographic boundaries, Population Estimates Program, Island Areas Census, urban/rural classification, and ACS | U.S. federal government data. Credit the U.S. Census Bureau and preserve the release vintage. Statistical boundaries are for data display. [Census terms](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/privacy/data_protection.html) |
| U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis state and territory GDP | Credit the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis and the original table. The state and territorial accounts have separate coverage and revisions. [BEA citation guidance](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/493) |
| World Bank World Development Indicators | Generally CC BY 4.0 under World Bank open data terms. Indicator metadata identifies third-party rights where applicable. Credit the World Bank and the original indicator source. [World Bank data terms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets) |
| Natural Earth cartographic subunits and hydrography | Public domain. StateAtlas credits Natural Earth. The subunits layer follows Natural Earth’s cartographic treatment of disputed areas. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) |
| Wikipedia excerpts and section indexes | Plain-text excerpts and headings attributed to linked article revisions under CC BY-SA 4.0. The atlas displays excerpts from the lead, with a live fallback when no local record exists. [Wikimedia terms](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use) |
| Wikimedia Commons flag artwork | Per-file attribution and reuse terms are recorded in [the flag catalogue](data/flags). StateAtlas uses Wikimedia-generated raster thumbnails. Check the original file page for reuse terms and any flag-specific restrictions. |

The [provenance manifest](public/datasets/manifest.json) identifies source URLs, vintages and input checksums.
